belleofthebot_AI concepts3 / 8
it is D

The system pursues something other than what was intended.

No villain. No malfunction. The system works exactly as built and the outcome is still bad.

belleofthebot_AI concepts4 / 8
three ways harm arrives
Misuse.A person deliberately uses a capable system to do damage.
Misalignment.The system pursues something other than what was meant.
Structural.Nobody misused it, nothing malfunctioned, and it still went badly.

The third is the one almost no coverage has a word for.

belleofthebot_AI concepts5 / 8
why the mix up wrecks the argument

Every plan that starts with “we just stop bad people using it” is aimed at one third of the problem.

The other two thirds need something other than a rule about who is allowed to press the button.

belleofthebot_AI concepts6 / 8
what we do not know

The two overlap in real incidents and there is usually no clean way to attribute a failure to one or the other. Most actual cases involve a person who wanted something and a system that supplied a version of it.
